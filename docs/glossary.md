# Glossary

The web app's plain explanations live in [glossary.ts](../apps/web/src/lib/glossary.ts) and are rendered by [Term](../apps/web/src/components/Term.tsx), with visible text and an accessible abbreviation that works without JavaScript. D1/D2 reuse those entries across pages; this document retains the project meanings and review limits below.

Terminology follows [PLAN](PLAN.md); every rule is checked against primary law in [rules sources](rules-sources.md) but has not been reviewed by a CA.

| Term                   | Meaning in this project                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| LUT (RFD-11)           | Letter of Undertaking used for the export-without-payment-of-IGST path; record its financial-year validity.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| FIRA / e-FIRA          | Foreign Inward Remittance Advice, including the digital document used to evidence foreign receipts and match them to payments.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| RBI purpose code       | A code describing the nature of a foreign receipt; recommendations come from a sourced table and require bank confirmation.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| GSTR-1 Table 6A        | Niryat prepares a ten-column WOPAY CSV for LUT-covered export invoices, with supplied INR/GST rates and separate refund reconciliation. Import into the GST offline tool is an accepted limitation (the tool rejected the synthetic test GSTIN before reading the file); nothing is filed automatically. |
| Export of services     | PLAN's five-condition checklist: supplier in India, recipient abroad, place of supply abroad, convertible-foreign-exchange receipt, and parties not merely establishments of one person. Implemented as a cited rule (IGST Act s. 2(6), s. 13(2)).                                                       |
| RBI realisation period | Time allowed for export proceeds to reach India: 9 months from the invoice date for services (12 if invoiced in INR), under the FEMA 2026 regulations from 1 Oct 2026. The period lives in the rules YAML, not in code.                                                                                  |
| Advance tax            | Income-tax payments due during the year, estimated using sourced rules rather than model-generated rates or dates.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Presumptive taxation   | Simplified scheme under Income-tax Act 2025 s. 58: 50% deemed income for specified professions within the ₹50 lakh / ₹75 lakh receipt limits. Niryat checks eligibility; it does not compute tax liability.                                                                                              |
| Credit note            | A document used to adjust a previously issued invoice, including relevant refund adjustments under verified GST rules.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ASMT-10                | A GST scrutiny notice concerning discrepancies; notice assistance is a stretch feature and not built.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| GSTIN                  | Goods and Services Tax Identification Number; sensitive profile data, encrypted at rest.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
